Clinical Pathology Reference

biotech

Glioma

Arising from glial cells within the CNS parenchyma. Highly infiltrative, making resection challenging.

Symptoms

Cognitive deficit, focal seizures, early morning headaches.

psychology

Meningioma

Typically benign extra-axial tumors originating from the arachnoid cap cells of the meningeal sheath.

Symptoms

Progressive focal deficits, visual disturbances, localized pain.

neurology

Pituitary

Adenomata located within the sella turcica, commonly causing visual chiasm compression and endocrine dysfunction.

Symptoms

Bitemporal hemianopsia, hormone dysregulation, headaches.

check_circle

Healthy

Uniform ventricular volume, symmetrical hemisphere structure, and absence of signal intensity anomalies.

Indicators

Standard sulci profile, clean tissue contrast, clear ventricles.
